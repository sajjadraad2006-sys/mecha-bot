# Mechatronics Engineering Curriculum — Stage 1 (المرحلة الأولى)

**Mechatronics Engineering Department — College of Engineering — University of Basrah**  
_Academic Program Specification & Standard Textbook Reference Guide_

---

## Overview

Stage 1 establishes the foundational engineering, physical, mathematical, and computational principles required for modern mechatronics systems.

---

## Semester 1 (الفصل الأول)

### 1. MCT111 — Mathematics I (الرياضيات 1)

- **Status**: Mandatory Department Core | **Units**: 3 | **Hours**: 3 Theory + 1 Tutorial
- **Course Description**:
  Functions, inverse functions, limits, continuity, differential calculus, techniques of differentiation, applications of derivatives (rates of change, optimization, curve sketching), introduction to integral calculus, indefinite and definite integrals, fundamental theorem of calculus.
- **Canonical Textbooks**:
  1. _Thomas' Calculus_ (14th or 15th Edition) — George B. Thomas, Maurice D. Weir, Joel Hass (Pearson).
  2. _Calculus: Early Transcendentals_ (9th Edition) — James Stewart (Cengage Learning).
- **Reference Scope**: `BIBLIOGRAPHIC_ONLY` / `OPEN_ACADEMIC_EQUIVALENT`

---

### 2. MCT112 — Electrical Circuits 1 (الدوائر الكهربائية 1)

- **Status**: Mandatory Department Core | **Units**: 3 | **Hours**: 2 Theory + 2 Lab + 1 Tutorial
- **Course Description**:
  Basic electrical circuit concepts, charge, current, voltage, power, and energy. Ohm's law, Kirchhoff's current law (KCL), Kirchhoff's voltage law (KVL). Single-loop and single-node-pair circuits. Resistor combinations, voltage and current division. Nodal analysis and Mesh analysis techniques. Network theorems: Superposition, Thevenin's theorem, Norton's theorem, Maximum power transfer theorem. Capacitors and inductors in DC circuits. Laboratory experiments covering practical circuit assembly, verification of KCL/KVL, and DC measurement instrumentation.
- **Canonical Textbooks**:
  1. _Engineering Circuit Analysis_ (9th Edition) — William H. Hayt, Jack E. Kemmerly, Steven M. Durbin (McGraw-Hill).
  2. _Electric Circuits_ (11th Edition) — James W. Nilsson, Susan A. Riedel (Pearson).
- **Reference Scope**: `BIBLIOGRAPHIC_ONLY` / `OPEN_ACADEMIC_EQUIVALENT`

---

### 3. MCT113 — Computer Principles (مبادئ الحاسوب)

- **Status**: Mandatory Department Core | **Units**: 2 | **Hours**: 2 Theory + 2 Lab
- **Course Description**:
  Fundamentals of computer systems, hardware architectures, microprocessors, memory hierarchy, input/output peripherals. Number systems (binary, octal, hexadecimal), binary arithmetic and data representation. Logic gates and Boolean logic foundation. Operating systems, file systems, introduction to procedural computer algorithms and flowcharts. Practical lab work on computer systems and office/engineering productivity tools.
- **Canonical Textbooks**:
  1. _Computer Science: An Overview_ (13th Edition) — J. Glenn Brookshear, Dennis Brylow (Pearson).
- **Reference Scope**: `BIBLIOGRAPHIC_ONLY`

---

### 4. MCT114 — Chemistry (الكيمياء)

- **Status**: General College Requirement | **Units**: 2 | **Hours**: 2 Theory + 2 Lab
- **Course Description**:
  General chemistry principles tailored for engineers. Atomic structure, chemical bonding, periodic table trends. Solutions and stoichiometry. Electrochemistry: oxidation-reduction reactions, galvanic cells, battery chemistries, and corrosion prevention. Engineering materials chemistry: polymers, semiconductors, lubricants, and fuel cells.
- **Canonical Textbooks**:
  1. _Chemistry for Engineering Students_ (4th Edition) — Lawrence S. Brown, Thomas A. Holme (Cengage Learning).
- **Reference Scope**: `BIBLIOGRAPHIC_ONLY`

---

### 5. MCT115 — Engineering Mechanics (Statics) (ميكانيك هندسي - ستاتيك)

- **Status**: Mandatory Department Core | **Units**: 3 | **Hours**: 3 Theory + 1 Tutorial
- **Course Description**:
  Force systems in 2D and 3D, vector operations on forces, moment of a force about a point and an axis. Resultant of force and moment systems. Equilibrium of particles and rigid bodies in two and three dimensions, free-body diagrams (FBD). Structural analysis: trusses (method of joints and method of sections), frames, and machines. Internal forces in structural members (axial force, shear force, bending moment). Dry friction (Coulomb friction) and wedge problems. Center of gravity, center of mass, and centroids of areas and composite shapes. Moments of inertia of areas and parallel-axis theorem.
- **Canonical Textbooks**:
  1. _Engineering Mechanics: Statics_ (14th or 15th Edition) — R. C. Hibbeler (Pearson).
  2. _Vector Mechanics for Engineers: Statics_ (12th Edition) — Ferdinand Beer, E. Russell Johnston, Jr. (McGraw-Hill).
- **Reference Scope**: `BIBLIOGRAPHIC_ONLY` / `OPEN_ACADEMIC_EQUIVALENT`

### 7. MCT117 — Engineering Drawing by Computer (الرسم الهندسي بالحاسوب)

- **Status**: Mandatory Department Core | **Units**: 2 | **Hours**: 1 Theory + 3 Lab
- **Course Description**:
  Principles of technical engineering drawing and geometric construction. Orthographic projections (first-angle and third-angle projections), isometric drawings, and sectional views. Dimensioning standards, tolerances, and engineering symbols. Practical implementation using computer-aided drafting software (AutoCAD / SolidWorks): 2D sketch drafting, modification commands, layers, layout setup, and plotting.
- **Canonical Textbooks**:
  1. _Technical Drawing with Engineering Graphics_ (15th Edition) — Frederick E. Giesecke, Alva Mitchell et al. (Pearson).
  2. _Engineering Drawing and Design_ (6th Edition) — David A. Madsen, David P. Madsen (Cengage).
- **Reference Scope**: `BIBLIOGRAPHIC_ONLY`

## Semester 2 (الفصل الثاني)

### 8. MCT121 — Mathematics II (الرياضيات 2)

- **Status**: Mandatory Department Core | **Units**: 3 | **Hours**: 3 Theory + 1 Tutorial
- **Course Description**:
  Techniques of integration (integration by parts, trigonometric integrals, partial fractions, trigonometric substitutions). Applications of definite integrals (areas between curves, volumes of solids of revolution). Infinite sequences and series, convergence tests, Taylor and Maclaurin series. Polar coordinates and parametric equations. Vectors in 3D space, lines and planes, dot and cross products.
- **Canonical Textbooks**:
  1. _Thomas' Calculus_ (14th/15th Edition) — George B. Thomas et al. (Pearson).
- **Reference Scope**: `BIBLIOGRAPHIC_ONLY`

---

### 9. MCT122 — Electrical Circuits 2 (الدوائر الكهربائية 2)

- **Status**: Mandatory Department Core | **Units**: 3 | **Hours**: 2 Theory + 2 Lab + 1 Tutorial
- **Course Description**:
  Sinusoidal alternating currents and voltages, frequency, amplitude, and phase angle. Phasors and complex impedance/admittance. AC circuit analysis using mesh and nodal methods in the frequency domain. AC circuit theorems (Superposition, Thevenin, Norton). Instantaneous power, average power, apparent power, reactive power, power factor and power factor correction. Series and parallel resonance, quality factor (Q) and bandwidth. Magnetically coupled circuits and linear transformers. Balanced three-phase systems (star and delta configurations), three-phase power measurement.
- **Canonical Textbooks**:
  1. _Electric Circuits_ (11th Edition) — James W. Nilsson, Susan A. Riedel (Pearson).
  2. _Engineering Circuit Analysis_ (9th Edition) — William H. Hayt et al. (McGraw-Hill).
- **Reference Scope**: `BIBLIOGRAPHIC_ONLY`

---

### 10. MCT123 — Digital Systems Design (تصميم النظم الرقمية)

- **Status**: Mandatory Department Core | **Units**: 3 | **Hours**: 2 Theory + 2 Lab + 1 Tutorial
- **Course Description**:
  Boolean algebra, switching algebra theorems, canonical and standard forms (SOP and POS). Karnaugh maps (K-maps) simplification for 2 to 5 variables, Don't-Care conditions. Combinational logic design: adders, subtractors, comparators, encoders, decoders, multiplexers, and demultiplexers. Sequential logic circuits: latches (SR, D), flip-flops (SR, D, JK, T), flip-flop excitation tables and timing diagrams. Analysis and design of synchronous and asynchronous counters, shift registers, state machines, and finite state machine (FSM) models. Practical laboratory implementation using TTL/CMOS ICs and logic simulators.
- **Canonical Textbooks**:
  1. _Digital Design: With an Introduction to the Verilog HDL_ (6th Edition) — M. Morris Mano, Michael D. Ciletti (Pearson).
  2. _Digital Fundamentals_ (11th Edition) — Thomas L. Floyd (Pearson).
- **Reference Scope**: `BIBLIOGRAPHIC_ONLY` / `OPEN_ACADEMIC_EQUIVALENT`

---

### 11. MCT124 — Physics (الفيزياء)

- **Status**: Mandatory Department Core | **Units**: 2 | **Hours**: 2 Theory + 2 Lab
- **Special Invariant Notice**: `MCT124 = CONFLICTING`. Flagged due to discrepancies in university documentation between classical physics (optics/electromagnetism) and semiconductor physics.
- **Course Description**:
  Wave motion, sound waves, physical optics (interference, diffraction, polarization). Fundamentals of electric fields, Gauss's law, electric potential, capacitance and dielectrics. Magnetic fields, Biot-Savart law, Ampere's law, Faraday's law of electromagnetic induction. Introduction to modern physics and semiconductor crystal structure.
- **Canonical Textbooks**:
  1. _Physics for Scientists and Engineers with Modern Physics_ (10th Edition) — Raymond A. Serway, John W. Jewett (Cengage).
  2. _University Physics with Modern Physics_ (15th Edition) — Hugh D. Young, Roger A. Freedman (Pearson).
- **Reference Scope**: `BIBLIOGRAPHIC_ONLY`

---

### 12. MCT125 — Engineering Mechanics (Dynamics) (ميكانيك هندسي - داينمك)

- **Status**: Mandatory Department Core | **Units**: 3 | **Hours**: 3 Theory + 1 Tutorial
- **Course Description**:
  Kinematics of particles: rectilinear motion, curvilinear motion (rectangular components, normal and tangential components, cylindrical components), relative motion. Kinetics of particles: Newton's second law of motion, equations of motion in various coordinate systems. Work, kinetic energy, potential energy, and principle of work and energy. Power and efficiency. Principle of linear impulse and momentum, conservation of linear momentum, direct and oblique central impact. Angular momentum. Planar kinematics and kinetics of rigid bodies.
- **Canonical Textbooks**:
  1. _Engineering Mechanics: Dynamics_ (14th/15th Edition) — R. C. Hibbeler (Pearson).
  2. _Vector Mechanics for Engineers: Dynamics_ (12th Edition) — Ferdinand Beer, E. Russell Johnston, Jr. (McGraw-Hill).
- **Reference Scope**: `BIBLIOGRAPHIC_ONLY`
