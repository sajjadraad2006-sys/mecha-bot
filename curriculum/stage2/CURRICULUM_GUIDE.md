# Mechatronics Engineering Curriculum — Stage 2 (المرحلة الثانية)

**Mechatronics Engineering Department — College of Engineering — University of Basrah**  
_Academic Program Specification & Standard Textbook Reference Guide_

---

## Overview

Stage 2 transitions students into core mechatronics electro-mechanical engineering: mechanics of materials, thermodynamics, DC/AC electrical machines, electronics, differential equations, and object-oriented programming.

---

## Semester 3 (الفصل الثالث)

### 1. MCT211 — Mathematics III (الرياضيات 3)

- **Status**: Mandatory Department Core | **Units**: 3 | **Hours**: 3 Theory + 1 Tutorial
- **Course Description**:
  Partial differentiation, chain rule, directional derivatives, gradient vectors, tangent planes, maxima and minima with Lagrange multipliers. Multiple integrals: double and triple integrals in Cartesian, cylindrical, and spherical coordinate systems, Jacobians. Vector calculus: vector fields, divergence, curl, line integrals, work, Green's theorem in the plane, surface integrals, divergence theorem of Gauss, and Stokes' theorem. Introduction to Fourier series (even and odd functions, half-range expansions).
- **Canonical Textbooks**:
  1. _Advanced Engineering Mathematics_ (10th Edition) — Erwin Kreyszig (John Wiley & Sons).
  2. _Thomas' Calculus: Early Transcendentals_ (14th/15th Edition) — George B. Thomas et al. (Pearson).
- **Reference Scope**: `BIBLIOGRAPHIC_ONLY` / `OPEN_ACADEMIC_EQUIVALENT`

---

### 2. MCT212 — Strength of Materials (مقاومة المواد)

- **Status**: Mandatory Department Core | **Units**: 3 | **Hours**: 2 Theory + 2 Lab + 1 Tutorial
- **Course Description**:
  Concepts of stress (normal and shear stress), strain, and stress-strain relationships. Hooke's law, modulus of elasticity, Poisson's ratio. Axially loaded members, thermal stresses, stress concentrations. Torsion of circular shafts, angle of twist, transmission of power. Shear force and bending moment diagrams for beams. Pure bending stress in symmetrical beams, flexure formula. Transverse shear stress in beams, shear flow. Compound stresses, Mohr's circle for plane stress and plane strain. Deflection of beams using double-integration and superposition methods. Introduction to column buckling (Euler's formula).
- **Canonical Textbooks**:
  1. _Mechanics of Materials_ (10th or 11th Edition) — R. C. Hibbeler (Pearson).
  2. _Mechanics of Materials_ (8th Edition) — Ferdinand Beer, E. Russell Johnston, Jr., John DeWolf, David Mazurek (McGraw-Hill).
- **Reference Scope**: `BIBLIOGRAPHIC_ONLY` / `OPEN_ACADEMIC_EQUIVALENT`

### 3. MCT213 — Metals Engineering (هندسة المعادن وعلم المواد)

- **Status**: Mandatory Department Core | **Units**: 2 | **Hours**: 2 Theory + 2 Lab
- **Course Description**:
  Atomic structure and interatomic bonding in solids. Crystal structures of metals (FCC, BCC, HCP), Miller indices for directions and planes. Imperfections in solids: point defects, line defects (dislocations), and interfacial defects. Mechanical properties of metals: tension, compression, hardness, impact, fatigue, and creep testing. Phase diagrams: Gibbs phase rule, binary isomorphous systems, eutectic systems, and the Iron-Iron Carbide (Fe-Fe3C) phase diagram. Heat treatment of steels: annealing, normalizing, quenching, and tempering. Surface hardening techniques. Ferrous and non-ferrous alloys, corrosion mechanisms and prevention in mechatronic mechanisms.
- **Canonical Textbooks**:
  1. _Materials Science and Engineering: An Introduction_ (10th Edition) — William D. Callister, Jr., David G. Rethwisch (John Wiley & Sons).
  2. _The Science and Engineering of Materials_ (7th Edition) — Donald R. Askeland, Wendelin J. Wright (Cengage).
- **Reference Scope**: `BIBLIOGRAPHIC_ONLY`

- **Status**: Mandatory Department Core | **Units**: 2 | **Hours**: 2 Theory + 2 Lab

Data structures (arrays, matrices, lists, dictionaries), algorithmic complexity. Vectorized computation, numerical arrays (NumPy), data manipulation, and 2D/3D visualization (Matplotlib). Symbolic mathematics and equation solving. Introduction to artificial intelligence concepts: state spaces, uninformed and informed search algorithms (A* search), regression models, classification foundations, and applications to sensor data analysis.

### 5. MCT215 — Electrical Machines (DC) (مكائن كهربائية - تيار مستمر)

- **Status**: Mandatory Department Core | **Units**: 2 | **Hours**: 2 Theory + 2 Lab
- **Course Description**:
  Principles of electromechanical energy conversion, magnetic circuits, flux density, reluctance, and magnetic saturation. DC machine construction, armature windings, commutator, and brushes. DC Generators: induced EMF, armature reaction, commutation, open-circuit and load characteristics, types (separately excited, shunt, series, and compound). DC Motors: principle of operation, back EMF, torque equation, torque-speed characteristics. Speed control methods of DC motors (armature voltage control, field flux control), starting methods and starters. Losses, efficiency, and industrial testing. DC servomotors and applications in mechatronic position and velocity control.
- **Canonical Textbooks**:
  1. _Electric Machinery Fundamentals_ (5th Edition) — Stephen J. Chapman (McGraw-Hill).
  2. _Electric Machines: Analysis and Design Applying MATLAB_ — Jim Cathey (McGraw-Hill).
- **Reference Scope**: `BIBLIOGRAPHIC_ONLY` / `OPEN_ACADEMIC_EQUIVALENT`

### 7. MCT217 — Thermodynamics (ديناميك الحرارة)

- **Status**: Mandatory Department Core | **Units**: 3 | **Hours**: 2 Theory + 1 Tutorial
- **Course Description**:
  Fundamental concepts of thermodynamics: systems, states, processes, cycles, temperature scales, pressure. Pure substances, phase change processes, P-v-T property diagrams and steam tables. Ideal gas equation of state and compressibility factor. The First Law of Thermodynamics: heat and work interactions, energy conservation for closed systems, internal energy, enthalpy, and specific heats. First Law analysis of open systems (control volumes): nozzles, diffusers, turbines, compressors, throttling valves, and heat exchangers. The Second Law of Thermodynamics: thermal energy reservoirs, heat engines, refrigerators, heat pumps, Kelvin-Planck and Clausius statements, the Carnot cycle. Concept of entropy, principle of entropy increase, isentropic efficiencies of engineering devices. Introduction to power cycles (Otto, Diesel, Rankine).
- **Canonical Textbooks**:
  1. _Thermodynamics: An Engineering Approach_ (9th or 10th Edition) — Yunus A. Cengel, Michael A. Boles, Mehmet Kanoglu (McGraw-Hill).
  2. _Fundamentals of Engineering Thermodynamics_ (9th Edition) — Michael J. Moran, Howard N. Shapiro et al. (Wiley).
- **Reference Scope**: `BIBLIOGRAPHIC_ONLY` / `OPEN_ACADEMIC_EQUIVALENT`

## Semester 4 (الفصل الرابع)

### 8. MCT221 — Differential Equations (المعادلات التفاضلية)

- **Status**: Mandatory Department Core | **Units**: 3 | **Hours**: 3 Theory + 1 Tutorial
- **Course Description**:
  First-order ordinary differential equations (ODEs): separable, linear, exact equations, integrating factors, Bernoulli equations, and orthogonal trajectories. Higher-order linear ODEs: homogeneous linear equations with constant coefficients, characteristic equations. Non-homogeneous equations: method of undetermined coefficients, variation of parameters. Laplace transforms: definition, linearity, first and second shifting theorems, transforms of derivatives and integrals, inverse Laplace transforms, partial fraction expansion, Dirac delta and unit step functions. Solving initial-value ODE problems via Laplace transforms. Systems of linear differential equations. Introduction to partial differential equations (heat, wave, and Laplace equations) and separation of variables.
- **Canonical Textbooks**:
  1. _Differential Equations with Boundary-Value Problems_ (9th Edition) — Dennis G. Zill (Cengage Learning).
  2. _Advanced Engineering Mathematics_ (10th Edition) — Erwin Kreyszig (Wiley).
- **Reference Scope**: `BIBLIOGRAPHIC_ONLY`

---

### 9. MCT222 — Electrical Machines (AC) (مكائن كهربائية - تيار متناوب)

- **Status**: Mandatory Department Core | **Units**: 3 | **Hours**: 2 Theory + 2 Lab + 1 Tutorial
- **Course Description**:
  Single-phase and three-phase transformers: construction, equivalent circuit, voltage regulation, efficiency, three-phase transformer connections. Three-phase induction motors: rotating magnetic field, construction (squirrel cage and wound rotor), slip, equivalent circuit, torque-speed characteristics, starting methods, speed control using Variable Frequency Drives (VFDs). Single-phase induction motors and starting mechanisms. Synchronous machines: construction, synchronous generators (alternators), phasor diagrams, voltage regulation. Synchronous motors: torque, power-angle curves, power factor correction. Specialized mechatronics motors: permanent magnet synchronous motors (PMSM), brushless DC (BLDC) motors, stepper motors (variable reluctance, permanent magnet, hybrid), and precision servo motors.
- **Canonical Textbooks**:
  1. _Electric Machinery Fundamentals_ (5th Edition) — Stephen J. Chapman (McGraw-Hill).
  2. _Principles of Electric Machines and Power Electronics_ (3rd Edition) — P. C. Sen (Wiley).
- **Reference Scope**: `BIBLIOGRAPHIC_ONLY` / `OPEN_ACADEMIC_EQUIVALENT`

### 10. MCT223 — Object-Oriented Programming (البرمجة الكيانية)

- **Status**: Mandatory Department Core | **Units**: 2 | **Hours**: 2 Theory + 2 Lab
- **Course Description**:
  Object-oriented software paradigm using C++. Classes and objects, data abstraction, encapsulation, access specifiers (public, private, protected). Constructors, destructors, copy constructors. Dynamic memory allocation (`new`, `delete`), pointers, and references. Operator overloading and friend functions. Inheritance: single, multiple, and multilevel inheritance, constructor chaining. Polymorphism: function overriding, virtual functions, pure virtual functions, and abstract base classes. Exception handling, input/output streams, file processing. Introduction to C++ Standard Template Library (STL): vectors, queues, stacks, iterators. Applications in embedded and robotic software modeling.
- **Canonical Textbooks**:
  1. _C++ How to Program_ (10th Edition) — Paul Deitel, Harvey Deitel (Pearson).
  2. _Object-Oriented Programming in C++_ (4th Edition) — Robert Lafore (Sams Publishing).
- **Reference Scope**: `BIBLIOGRAPHIC_ONLY`

---

### 11. MCT224 — Theory of Machines (نظرية الآلات والميكانيزمات)

- **Status**: Mandatory Department Core | **Units**: 3 | **Hours**: 2 Theory + 1 Tutorial
- **Course Description**:
  Kinematics of planar mechanisms, links, kinematic pairs, kinematic chains, degrees of freedom (Grübler and Kutzbach criteria). Inversion of four-bar linkages and slider-crank mechanisms. Graphical and analytical velocity analysis: instantaneous centers of rotation, relative velocity method. Acceleration analysis: normal, tangential, and Coriolis acceleration components. Cam mechanisms: follower motion types (uniform, simple harmonic, cycloidal), cam profile synthesis. Gears and gear trains: law of gearing, conjugate profiles, spur gear geometry, epicyclic (planetary) gear trains, speed ratio, and torque analysis. Static and dynamic force analysis of mechanisms, flywheel design, balancing of rotating and reciprocating masses. Gyroscopic motion and precessional torque.
- **Canonical Textbooks**:
  1. _Theory of Machines and Mechanisms_ (5th Edition) — John J. Uicker, Gordon R. Pennock, Joseph E. Shigley (Oxford University Press).
  2. _Mechanics of Machines_ (2nd Edition) — William L. Cleghorn, Nikolai Dechev (Oxford University Press).
- **Reference Scope**: `BIBLIOGRAPHIC_ONLY` / `OPEN_ACADEMIC_EQUIVALENT`

---

### 12. MCT225 — Electronics (الإلكترونيات)

- **Status**: Mandatory Department Core | **Units**: 3 | **Hours**: 2 Theory + 2 Lab + 1 Tutorial
- **Course Description**:
  Semiconductor physics review, p-n junction diodes, diode equivalent circuits, I-V characteristics. Diode applications: half-wave, full-wave center-tapped, and bridge rectifiers; capacitor filters; voltage regulation using Zener diodes; clippers and clampers. Bipolar Junction Transistors (BJT): npn and pnp operations, transistor characteristics, DC biasing circuits (fixed bias, emitter-stabilized, voltage divider bias), stability factors. BJT small-signal AC analysis: re model, hybrid equivalent model, common-emitter (CE), common-base (CB), and common-collector (CC) amplifiers. Field-Effect Transistors (FET): JFET and MOSFET (depletion and enhancement types), operation, transfer characteristics, DC biasing, and small-signal amplifiers. Operational Amplifiers (Op-Amps): ideal op-amp characteristics, inverting, non-inverting, summing, difference, differentiator, and integrator circuits. Active filters and comparators.
- **Canonical Textbooks**:
  1. _Electronic Devices and Circuit Theory_ (11th Edition) — Robert L. Boylestad, Louis Nashelsky (Pearson).
  2. _Microelectronic Circuits_ (8th Edition) — Adel S. Sedra, Kenneth C. Smith (Oxford University Press).
- **Reference Scope**: `BIBLIOGRAPHIC_ONLY` / `OPEN_ACADEMIC_EQUIVALENT`
